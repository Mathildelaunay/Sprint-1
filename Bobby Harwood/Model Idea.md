I believe that there is a correlation between the rankings of the ratios of goals scored to goals conceded for each Premier League team in a season and that same season's actual final ranking table. The idea of my model is to be able to predict the total number of goals scored by each team over a single season, then do the same for goals conceded, calculate the ratio of these two (total goals scored divided by total goals conceded), and then finally rank these teams from highest ratio to lowest ratio which will be our prediction for the final league table for the 2026/27 Premier League season.
